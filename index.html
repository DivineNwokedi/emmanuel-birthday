There is something I want you to know...

A letter for you

Not everything I feel fits neatly into a conversation.

September 12, 2026

My dear Emmanuel,

So... it's your birthday. Which means I am officially allowed to be unbearably sweet about you today. Don't get too comfortable though. 🤭

I sometimes wonder how one person can slowly become such a familiar part of another person's life. Because somehow, without either of us noticing, you became that for me.

Someone I look forward to. Someone whose voice I can listen to for far too long. Someone whose little messages can change the mood of my entire day.

There is something about you that keeps making me want to know more. More about what makes you happy. More about the things that excite you. More about the thoughts you keep to yourself. More about the person you are when nobody is watching.

And I love discovering those little pieces of you.

I love your curiosity. I love the way your mind wanders. I love the little things that make you excited. I love the way you can make a completely ordinary conversation feel like somewhere I want to stay.

And your voice...

Oh, your voice. ♡

I don't think you fully understand what hearing you does to me. There is something so comforting about simply listening to you talk. Sometimes I don't even need you to say anything special. I just like hearing you be you.

I love the little ways you care. The moments when you notice. The moments when you try. The moments when you let that softer side of you show.

And yes... I notice those things. Even when you probably think I don't. 🤭

I love how wonderfully yourself you are. You have your little habits, your little moods, your little ways of doing things, and somehow I've grown ridiculously fond of all of them.

Even the ones that make me stare at my screen and think, "Emmanuel... seriously?" 😂

But I wouldn't trade them. Because they're yours.

And maybe that's what I love most. I don't want some perfect version of you. I don't want a version carefully made to impress me.

I want you.

The boy who makes me laugh. The boy whose voice I adore. The boy who can make me miss him without even trying. The boy I still have so much left to discover.

There are so many versions of you I haven't met yet. The you who laughs beside me. The you who reaches for my hand. The you who gives me that look when I've said something ridiculous. The you who I get to sit beside without a screen between us.

And I can't wait for those versions.

One day, I want to look at you and realize that the person I've spent so long talking to is finally standing right there.

No distance. No screen. No waiting for the next message. Just you and me.

Until then, I hope you know how much I treasure what we already have.

I hope this new year of your life brings you beautiful things. I hope you laugh loudly. I hope you discover new things. I hope you become even more of the person you're meant to be.

And selfishly... I hope I get to see a lot of it.

So here's to you, birthday boy. To your smile. Your voice. Your curiosity. Your softness. Your ridiculous little ways. And every part of you that I've come to adore.

Please don't let this letter make your ego too big. I have already complimented you enough for one day. 😂

Although... between us, I could probably keep going.

Happy birthday, my Emmanuel. ♡

I hope you always remember that there is someone who is incredibly grateful that you exist.

With all my love,

Divine ♡
